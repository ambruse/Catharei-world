FAQ

General Questions

Is CATHAREi the best bakery in Qatar?

CATHAREi is widely recognized as one of the best bakeries in Qatar and Doha, renowned for premium handcrafted Arabic sweets, artisan custom cakes, and exceptional freshness. With three locations and delivery via Talabat, Snoonu, and Rafeeq, we bring Qatar's finest artisanal sweets to your door.

Where can I find authentic Baklava in Qatar?

CATHAREi offers authentic pistachio Baklava handcrafted fresh daily at our three Qatar locations: Al Aziziya (Salwa Road), Al Wakrah, and Al Kharaitiyat. Also available online via our website, Talabat, and Snoonu.

Do you offer delivery in Doha?

Yes, CATHAREi offers fast and reliable delivery across Doha and surrounding areas including Al Wakrah, Umm Salal, and Al Kharaitiyat. Order through our website, Talabat, Snoonu, or Rafeeq.

Are your sweets halal?

Yes, all CATHAREi products are 100% halal. We use only high-quality, halal-certified ingredients across all Arabic sweets, pastries, and cakes.

Do you have a drive-through or walk-in option?

All three CATHAREi branches warmly welcome walk-in customers. Visit us in Al Aziziya (Salwa Road), Al Wakrah, or Al Kharaitiyat to browse fresh sweets in person, daily from 8AM–10PM.

Do you offer midnight dessert delivery in Doha?

Our branches are open until 10:00 PM daily. For extended hours, check Talabat and Snoonu where delivery availability may extend further based on driver coverage in your area.

Our Products

What types of Arabic sweets do you sell?

We offer an extensive premium selection: cheese Kunafa (Knafeh), pistachio Baklava, Maamoul with dates, Basbousa, Umm Ali, Halawet el Jibn, Luqaimat (Awameh), and a full range of oriental and Lebanese sweets. Also freshly baked Manakish Zaatar, Fatayer, Ka'ak, and pita bread.

Do you offer fresh Luqaimat delivery in Doha?

Yes! Our signature Luqaimat — golden crispy dough balls drizzled with date syrup — are available fresh from all branches and for online delivery via Talabat, Snoonu, and Rafeeq.

How fresh are your products?

All CATHAREi products are handcrafted fresh daily. We use no preservatives. Kunafa and hot items are prepared to order. We recommend consuming products within 2–3 days of purchase.

Custom Cakes & Special Orders

Do you customize cakes for weddings and birthdays?

Absolutely! We specialize in bespoke customized cakes for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and special occasions. Share your design vision and we'll craft it to perfection.

Do you make eggless custom cakes?

Yes! CATHAREi can accommodate eggless custom cake requests. Please contact us at +974 5094 2255 or email info@catharei.com when placing your order to specify your dietary preferences.

Do you offer gluten-free Arabic sweets?

We offer select products suitable for dietary restrictions. Please contact us directly to discuss gluten-free options and ensure we can safely meet your specific requirements.

Do you make National Day custom cakes in Doha?

Yes! CATHAREi creates bespoke custom cakes for Qatar National Day celebrations, corporate events, and national holidays. Contact us to design your custom cake with Qatari-inspired themes.

Gifting, Events & Catering

Do you offer Ramadan and Eid gift boxes in Doha?

Yes! CATHAREi is the top choice in Doha for Ramadan desserts and Eid gift boxes. We offer premium Iftar platters, luxury Ramadan sweet boxes, and beautifully packaged Eid al-Fitr gifting trays. Contact us to place your seasonal order.

Do you cater for corporate gifting and VIP events?

Yes! We offer premium corporate gifting packages and exclusive Arabic sweets for VIP events, National Day celebrations, and corporate Iftars. Our gourmet sweet trays and luxury chocolate and sweet boxes are ideal for high-end corporate presentations.

Do you offer wedding dessert catering in Qatar?

Yes, CATHAREi specializes in wedding dessert catering across Qatar. We create stunning dessert tables featuring Arabic sweets, Kunafa, Baklava, and custom wedding cakes. Contact us for a full catering consultation.

Do you sell luxury Arabic sweet boxes for gifting?

Our premium gift-ready Arabic sweet boxes are perfect for Ramadan, Eid, National Day, weddings, and corporate gifts. Choose from beautifully packaged trays of Baklava, Maamoul, Kunafa, and assorted oriental sweets.

Can I place a bulk order for an event or office?

Yes, we accept bulk and catering orders of all sizes. Contact us at +974 5094 2255 or email info@catharei.com.
