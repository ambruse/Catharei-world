Catharei website SEO audit

Capture date: 1 November 2026 (Asia/Riyadh). Site: www.catharei.com. Working goal: improve qualified discovery and orders for sweets, cakes, catering and branch visits in Qatar, in English and Arabic. Goal and market inferred from the site and this chat.

What matters first

No unintended site-wide indexing blocker was found in the bounded sample. Start with oversized product images and conflicting branch facts, then repair copied schema and broken schema references. Complete Arabic content and useful buying information before expanding landing pages. No production SEO changes were made.

What is already working

The 16 sampled sitemap landing pages returned HTTP 200, with individual titles and descriptions, one H1, self-referencing canonicals and no observed noindex. Their JSON-LD parsed. Catalogue source HTML contains product content and links. HTTP/non-www homepage requests resolved to HTTPS www. A deliberately nonexistent page returned 404. Login is intentionally noindex and is not treated as a public landing-page defect. Robots allows general crawling and disallows /api/; catalogue content was available in source.

No horizontal overflow or page JavaScript exceptions appeared in the six rendered views. All sampled HTML img elements had alt attributes, although attribute presence alone does not establish description quality. The sampled article includes organizational attribution, dates, a relevant cultural source and useful menu links.

Findings and action plan

F01 · High — Reduce oversized catalogue images

At 390 × 844, the Arabic menu recorded 10,370,061 resource transfer bytes and Mini Sandwich recorded 4,782,458. Seven menu JPEGs were approximately 0.98–1.75 MB each. These are single-run resource diagnostics, not field performance or CWV measurements.

/menu.html?lang=ar · /menu/mini-sandwich-2

Evidence: rendered-evidence.json: resourceBytes; resource-sample.json: resources

Evidence level: Rendered observation

Confidence: High

Action: Generate appropriately sized WebP/AVIF derivatives; use srcset/sizes, explicit dimensions and below-fold lazy loading. Keep suitable priority imagery for the first viewport. Fix the upload pipeline so new images stay optimized.

Owner: Frontend/backend developer

Dependency: Preserve originals and verify derivative quality.

Planning effort: 1–2 developer days

Success check: Repeat fresh-context visits at the same viewport and delay; demonstrate reduced image transfer and intact imagery, then collect separate mobile lab and field baselines.

F02 · High — Reconcile branch hours

Contact-page schema gives Salwa Road 06:00–23:00 and other branches 07:00–23:00. Delivery FAQ schema says all branches operate 08:00–22:00. The two inspected branch pages ask visitors to call for current hours. The correct schedule is unknown.

/contact.html · /delivery.html · /locations/al-aziziya.html · /locations/al-wakrah.html

Evidence: Contact snapshot lines 64–136; delivery snapshot lines 94 and 118; branch Hours text

Evidence level: Source HTML

Confidence: High

Action: Obtain one approved schedule per branch, including exceptions; publish it visibly and use the same source in structured data. Remove unsupported schedules until verified. Compare Business Profiles when access is available.

Owner: Business owner + developer

Dependency: Owner-approved hours; Business Profile access for later cross-check.

Planning effort: 0.5–1 day after facts are supplied

Success check: No conflicting hours; all marked-up schedules match visible approved information.

F03 · Medium — Replace copied menu schema

The blog index and contact page both identify themselves as the full menu in CollectionPage JSON-LD and point its url to /menu.html. Their titles and canonicals correctly describe their own pages.

/blog · /contact.html

Evidence: www.catharei.com_blog.html:35; www.catharei.com_contact.html.html:35

Evidence level: Source HTML

Confidence: High

Action: Generate a page-specific Blog/CollectionPage and ContactPage with their own identity and URL, or remove the unrelated block. Preserve valid business entities.

Owner: Developer

Dependency: None

Planning effort: 1–3 hours

Success check: Source and rendered JSON-LD describe the current page without the copied menu entity.

F04 · Medium — Repair broken schema references

Both sampled branch breadcrumbs point to /locations/, which returned 404. All three storefront JPEG URLs in contact-page Bakery markup also returned 404. These are schema-reference defects; a broken visible navigation link was not established.

/locations/al-aziziya.html · /locations/al-wakrah.html · /contact.html

Evidence: followup-evidence.json: /locations/ and three storefront URLs; branch BreadcrumbList; contact Bakery image fields

Evidence level: HTTP response + source HTML

Confidence: High

Action: Use an existing relevant branch/contact hub in breadcrumbs or build the intended hub. Replace image URLs with real appropriate branch images; omit unsupported optional references rather than inventing assets.

Owner: Developer + content owner

Dependency: Approved branch imagery and branch-hub decision.

Planning effort: 2–4 hours if assets exist

Success check: Referenced URLs return 200 with suitable content; breadcrumb hierarchy and branch images accurately describe the business.

F05 · Medium — Complete Arabic content before expanding Arabic search targeting

The homepage H1 translates in the rendered Arabic view. On /menu.html?lang=ar, html lang becomes ar but the main heading and entry CTA stay English. Both tested Arabic URLs retain English titles, English-first source, base-page canonicals and no hreflang. This is currently a language toggle, not a complete set of localized search landing pages.

/?lang=ar · /menu.html?lang=ar

Evidence: crawl-evidence.json: titles, canonicals, hreflang; rendered-evidence.json; render_menu_html_lang_ar-390.png

Evidence level: Source HTML + rendered observation

Confidence: High

Action: Translate remaining headings, controls and meaningful product details. If separate Arabic search landing pages are intended, create fully translated stable URLs with appropriate canonicals and reciprocal language annotations. Do not change canonicals or add hreflang merely because a toggle exists.

Owner: Arabic editor + developer

Dependency: Arabic search strategy and approved translations.

Planning effort: 2–5 days for a small pilot

Success check: Fresh browsers show complete Arabic content and metadata; URL, canonical and language annotations consistently implement the selected strategy.

F06 · Medium opportunity — Enable accurate variant product offers

Mini Sandwich and Fatayer show size prices but Product JSON-LD omits offers, review and aggregateRating. Eclair Mix Box has an Offer matching the visible QAR 75 price. Local catalogue-seo.js deliberately omits offers for variants because individually selectable URLs are not implemented. This is an intentional limitation, not malformed JSON.

/menu/mini-sandwich-2 · /menu/fatayer-67 · /menu/eclair-mix-box-4

Evidence: Captured Product JSON-LD in initial/follow-up evidence; ../catalogue-seo.js:107–115

Evidence level: Source HTML + local implementation context

Confidence: High

Action: Design selectable, addressable variants backed by truthful price/availability data, then implement appropriate product/offer markup. Do not invent reviews or use AggregateOffer as a shortcut for size variants. Validate against current Google requirements.

Owner: Developer + catalogue owner

Dependency: Variant selection and URL model; reliable offer data.

Planning effort: 1–3 days

Success check: Selected size, visible price, URL and JSON-LD agree; Rich Results Test confirms required fields. Eligibility does not guarantee display.

F07 · Medium — Verify service claims and answer buying questions

Delivery and corporate-gifting pages make dietary, delivery-speed, range and customer-trust claims not substantiated by the inspected material. Sample products route ingredients, allergens, portions and preparation questions to a branch. This is an evidence gap, not proof that claims are false.

/delivery.html · /corporate-gifting.html · /menu/mini-sandwich-2 · /menu/eclair-mix-box-4

Evidence: Delivery snapshot:272,286; corporate-gifting snapshot:259–264; product main content

Evidence level: Source HTML + editorial assessment

Confidence: High for observed gap; underlying business facts unknown

Action: Have operations verify dietary and delivery claims. Add approved portions, ingredients/allergens, lead times, delivery conditions and gifting minimums where applicable. Replace repeated superlatives with useful specifics and verified examples.

Owner: Operations/catalogue owner + editor

Dependency: Verified product/service facts and consent for customer proof.

Planning effort: 1–3 editorial days for priority pages after fact collection

Success check: Consequential claims have an internal fact source; priority buying questions have clear on-page answers without invented guarantees.

Implementation sequence

  1. Confirm branch hours and service claims (F02/F07) while preparing image derivatives and the upload fix (F01). Set measurement baselines (F08).
  2. Correct page schema (F03) and repair breadcrumb/image references after asset and hub decisions (F04).
  3. Pilot complete Arabic pages (F05), addressable variants (F06), and richer buying information (F07). Validate before extending across the catalogue.

Effort ranges are planning assumptions for an existing codebase and exclude waiting for facts, translations and assets. They are not delivery commitments.

Remaining uncertainty

No Search Console, GA4, Business Profile, field CWV, ranking or backlink data was supplied. There was no Google URL Inspection, Rich Results Test or Lighthouse run. Rich-result observations compare source data against current documentation and do not certify eligibility. Business hours, dietary information and service promises need owner verification. Unvisited sitemap URLs and most link/asset destinations remain unknown. No traffic growth, conversion lift, ranking penalty or complete index coverage is claimed.

Source-readable content, attribution and public crawl access support agent usability; AI citation visibility was not measured. The live llms.txt returned 200, but its presence is not scored. Google's AI-search guidance relies on ordinary SEO fundamentals rather than special AI markup.
